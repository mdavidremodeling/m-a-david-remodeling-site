What Sets Us Apart

On-site measurement. Complete project. Every detail documented.

Before any render is drawn, we go to the site. Rosangela personally measures the space and prepares the full technical documentation required to file for Permit with the city where the project is located — the step most design studios leave out.

  • In-person site visit and technical survey
  • Complete documentation for municipal Permit approval
  • Follow-through until the city issues final approval
