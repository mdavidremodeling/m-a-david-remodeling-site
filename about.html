About

One vision, two companies, one shared commitment.

I'm a partner in both M David Remodeling and Art Design — the first leads design, the second carries out construction. I brought the two together so my clients never have to bridge the gap between separate offices: one team follows every project from concept to final delivery.

Before a single drawing is made, I go to the site myself to measure and survey the space. That care is what the rest of the process rests on — an accurate project, complete documentation, and a clear path to Permit approval with the city where the work takes place.

I work with discerning clients across Florida who want more than a beautiful render — they want a project that actually gets built, legally and on schedule.

How We Work

From on-site survey to finished construction

A four-step process, led start to finish by the same team.

Step 01

On-Site Measurement

An in-person visit to measure and assess the space and its conditions.

Step 02

Design & Planning

Concept development, floor plans, elevations and 3D renderings.

Step 03

Municipal Permit

Documentation prepared and followed through with the city until approval.

Step 04

Construction & Execution

Full site management through to final delivery, held to the same standard as the design.
